# 山东本科院校介绍 · Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Editorial map-card motion graphics for a Chinese education guide: deep navy canvas, coastal cyan accents, warm gold highlights, crisp white typography, subtle topographic lines and grid marks. The mood is confident, informative, and regionally grounded rather than promotional. Cards should feel like a clean admissions atlas: strong hierarchy, generous spacing, restrained motion, and clear labels.

## Colors

- `#071B2D` — deep navy canvas
- `#0F3D56` — secondary panel / map field
- `#19C3B1` — coastal cyan accent
- `#F4B942` — warm gold emphasis
- `#F4F7F8` — primary text

## Typography

- `Arial, "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", sans-serif` — Chinese UI and body text
- `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif` — small editorial eyebrow only

## What NOT to Do

- No default Swiss-gray background or generic blue gradient.
- No dense paragraphs; keep each card scannable in under four seconds.
- No forced `<br>` line breaks in body copy.
- No jump cuts between scenes; every scene uses an entrance and a wipe transition.
- Do not present rankings, admission scores, or employment claims unless separately sourced.
