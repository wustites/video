This November, the Asia-Pacific comes together in Shenzhen. From November eighteenth to nineteenth, twenty twenty-six, the city hosts the thirty-third APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting.

Founded in nineteen eighty-nine, APEC brings together twenty-one economies around the Pacific Rim. They are home to nearly forty percent of the world's people, and more than sixty percent of its economic output.

China hosts APEC for the third time, after Shanghai in two thousand one and Beijing in twenty fourteen. The theme of the year is Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together.

Three priorities guide the agenda. Openness, to keep trade and investment flowing. Innovation, to unlock new growth. And cooperation, to share the benefits across the region.

Why Shenzhen? Forty-six years ago, it was a small border town. As one of China's first special economic zones, it grew into a global hub of technology, manufacturing and trade.

Today, Shenzhen is a city of more than seventeen million people, home to world-leading companies in electronics, telecommunications and electric vehicles. It sits at the heart of the Greater Bay Area, linked to Hong Kong and Macao.

A city built on openness now welcomes the Asia-Pacific. Shenzhen, twenty twenty-six. Together, toward a shared future.
